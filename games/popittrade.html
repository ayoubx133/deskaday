About the game

Run your own toy stall at a sunny market. Stickman kids visit one by one and offer a trade: one of their fidget toys for one of yours. Look at the WANTED card and the line of kids still coming, then say DEAL or NO to end up with every wanted toy. Each new toy is tested right away: pop the bubbles, spin the spinner and squish the squishy.

  • 30 clever trading puzzles plus endless market days
  • Pop, spin and squish 12 colorful fidget toys
  • Free hints, level map and 6 seller outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
