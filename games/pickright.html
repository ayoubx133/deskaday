About the game

Each level shows a stickman in a funny little trouble, like a stuck balloon, a melting snowman or a dark room. Read the question and tap one of the three item cards at the bottom. The right item fixes the problem with a happy surprise, while the wrong ones end in silly fails, so think before you tap! Use the free hint bulb when you are stuck, earn coins and stars, and unlock fun outfits for your stickman.

  • 60 handcrafted situations with clever twists
  • Funny outcomes for every wrong item
  • Free hints, level select and 6 stickman skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
