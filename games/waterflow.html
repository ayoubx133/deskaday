About the game

Swipe your finger through the sand to dig tunnels and let the water flow. Lead every drop to the thirsty plants so they bloom, and fill the bathtub for the stickman who is waiting for a bath. Rocks cannot be dug, so find a way around them, and keep the water away from the sleeping cat. Use as few swipes as you can for three stars.

  • 30 calm garden puzzles with flowing water
  • Dig anywhere with your finger, rocks block the way
  • Free hints show where to dig next
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
