About the game

Every level is a new little story with a short question, like wake him up or find 5 stars. Drag things around, rub clouds away, shake trees, tilt the room, turn boxes and even pull letters out of the question. The answer is never the obvious one, so look closely and think in a silly way. Stuck? Tap the free hint bulb and the stickman will show you the trick.

  • 40 handmade trick levels with funny answers
  • Drag, rub, shake, tilt and turn to solve
  • Free hints and 6 fun stickman outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
