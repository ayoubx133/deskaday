About the game

Your roommate is snoring, so it is prank time! Drag items slowly, tap, hold and wiggle to do each silly prank step by step, like a feather tickle, a confetti fan or a duck parade on his blanket. Watch the noise meter: fast moves, squeaky toys and creaky boards make noise, and loud steps only work during his big snore. Finish the prank without waking him to earn stars and coins for fun new looks.

  • 32 handmade silly pranks with funny endings
  • Noise meter, big snore timing and free hints
  • Unlock 6 cute looks like ninja, kitty and dino
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
