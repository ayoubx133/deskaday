About the game

Every stickman wants to go home to the house with his color. Draw a path from each stickman to his house, and as soon as the last path is ready they all start walking at the same time and at the same speed. Paths that cross are fine, but two stickmen at the same spot at the same moment go bonk, so add a little loop or a detour to make one of them arrive later. Watch out for rolling beach balls, waddling ducks and cars on the road, and use the free hint any time you get stuck.

  • 32 handmade timing puzzles with up to four stickmen
  • Rolling balls, ducks, cars, ponds, hedges and fences
  • Free hints, level map and 6 fun outfits to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
