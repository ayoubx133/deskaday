About the game

You have a few seconds to draw one shield before the storm starts. Your line drops into the scene as a solid shape, then rain, popcorn and snowballs fall for 8 seconds. Keep the ice cream, the snowman and the sleepy cat dry to win. Lines drawn through a nail hang from it like an umbrella, and using less ink gives more stars.

  • 36 handmade storm puzzles with real physics
  • Rain, wind, popcorn meteors, snowballs and nails
  • Six stickman outfits and crayon colors
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
