About the game

Somebody ate the cake! Search the cartoon bakery for hidden clues like a muddy footprint, a jam spot, a torn ribbon, a feather or cake crumbs. Every clue tells you something about the snacker, such as brown boots or a feather hat. When the stickman suspects line up, tap the one who matches every clue, then watch them blush, say sorry and share the treat.

  • 30 funny kid mysteries plus endless cases
  • Find clues, compare suspects and solve the case
  • Free hints, case map and 6 detective outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
