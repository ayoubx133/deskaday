About the game

The stickman wants to drive his little car home. Draw a road with your finger over gaps, hills and walls, then press GO and watch the car drive on your road with real bumpy physics. Collect the three stars on the way and park inside the garage to finish the level. Make jumps, dips and bridges, but do not let the car drop into the water or get stuck, and use the free hint when you need an idea.

  • 32 handmade road drawing puzzles
  • Real car physics with jumps, dips and bridges
  • Free hints, level map and 6 colorful cars
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
