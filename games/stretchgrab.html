About the game

Drag the stickman's stretchy hand along a clever path to grab gems, trophies and crowns in a silly cartoon museum. Snake around laser beams, slip past sweeping cameras and never touch the dozing guard. Time the blinking and moving lasers, then let go to zip the arm back with the loot. Free hints show the path, and 32 handmade levels get trickier step by step.

  • 32 handmade stretchy arm puzzles with a free hint path
  • Lasers, cameras, glass cases and a sleepy guard to outsmart
  • Unlock 6 funny stickman skins with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
