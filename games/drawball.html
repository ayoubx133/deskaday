About the game

Draw one line and it becomes a solid ramp. Lift your finger and the ball drops, rolls along your line and flies toward the cup. Use bounce pads, portals, fans and moving cups to reach tricky spots. Less ink earns three stars, the light bulb shows a hint and retry is instant.

  • 36 handmade ball puzzles with real physics
  • Bounce pads, portals, fans and moving cups
  • Six stickman outfits with matching ball and crayon
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
