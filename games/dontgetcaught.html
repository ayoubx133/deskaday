About the game

Hold the screen to secretly eat candy, play your game or blow a big bubble while the teacher, mom or the sleepy guard looks away. Let go the moment they cough, squeak or start to turn around, and your stickman whistles like nothing happened. If they see you, the suspicion eye fills up and you get caught, so watch for fake turns, mirrors and flashlights. Fill the fun bar to win the level and collect coins for fun new outfits.
