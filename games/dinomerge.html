About the game

Buy dino eggs and watch cute baby dinos hatch on your grid. Drag two of the same dino together to merge them into a bigger, stronger one, from baby dino all the way to the rainbow dino. When your squad is ready, press FIGHT and cheer as your dinos bonk the rival squad in a silly cartoon battle with stars and no scary stuff. Plan your merges, beat 30 squads and unlock fun coach outfits.

  • 12 cute dinos to hatch and merge
  • 30 rival squads plus endless remix battles
  • Free hints, level map and 6 coach outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
