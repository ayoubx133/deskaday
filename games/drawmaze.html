About the game

A sneaky stickman wants a slice of the big strawberry cake in the bakery. Draw one path from him to the cake, then watch him tiptoe along it at a steady speed. The bakers sweep their flashlights from side to side, walk up and down or spin around, and the light stops at tables and shelves, so hide behind the furniture and time your path. Add a little loop to wait for the light to turn away, never wake a napping baker, and save ink for three stars.

  • 32 handmade sneaky path puzzles
  • Sweeping flashlights, patrols, cameras and napping bakers
  • Free hints, level map and 6 sneaky skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
