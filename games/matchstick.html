About the game

Each board shows a wrong equation built from matchstick digits. Drag one or two sticks to a new spot so the sum becomes true. Some boards ask you to make a number of squares instead. Press reset any time, and tap the hint bulb to see which stick to move.

  • 50 matchstick puzzles checked by a solver
  • Easy sums for kids up to tricky two stick moves
  • Free hints, reset button and a level map
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
