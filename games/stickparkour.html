About the game

Your stickman runs on his own across the city rooftops, so all you do is time your moves. Tap to jump over gaps and crates, tap again in the air for a double jump flip, and swipe down to slide under laundry lines and low signs. Jump into tall walls to wall-run up and pop over the top, and grab coins and stars on the way to the finish flag. Clear 30 stages, then chase your best distance in Endless mode and unlock fun new outfits.

  • 30 rooftop stages plus an Endless mode
  • Jump, double jump flips, slides and wall-runs
  • 6 cool stickman outfits to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
