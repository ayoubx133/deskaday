About the game

Grandma is busy at the stove and the cookies are right there on the shelf. Drag the stickman's stretchy arm to grab cookies, cupcakes and cake, and pull it back under the table before she turns around. Watch out for the playful cat paw and the swinging cupboard doors. 32 handmade kitchen levels with free hints and silly reactions.
