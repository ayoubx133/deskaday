About the game

Tap a golden pin to slide it out and watch water, sand, gems and hot goo pour down the cave. Water cools the orange goo into harmless stone, and a splash of water washes the silly slime blob away. Pick the right order so every gem rolls into the stickman's treasure sack. Stuck? Tap the light bulb for a free hint or retry instantly.

  • 40 handmade cave puzzles with real flowing water and gems
  • Free hints, instant retry and a level map
  • Six fun stickman outfits to unlock with coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
