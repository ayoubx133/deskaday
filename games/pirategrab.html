About the game

Sneak a stretchy pirate arm through the cabins of a rocking ship and grab gold coins, gems, crowns and maps. The ship rocks on a steady rhythm, so lanterns swing, barrels roll and treasure slides across the tables. Stay out of the parrot's squawk rings and never touch the snoring captain. Watch the waves, pick your moment and draw a smart path with your finger.

  • 36 handmade pirate puzzles with a level map
  • Rocking ship: swinging lanterns, rolling barrels, sliding loot
  • Free hints that show the path, plus 6 pirate skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
