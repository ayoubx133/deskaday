About the game

The stickman DJ shows a little dance on the disco stage. Remember the order of the moves, then tap the matching pose cards to copy the dance with your own stickman. Each level adds more moves and a faster beat. You have three hearts, and a free slow motion hint shows the dance again.

  • 40 dance memory levels with growing sequences
  • Disco music with a note for every move
  • Six dancer skins to unlock with coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
