About the game

Look at the clue scales to work out how heavy each fruit is. Drag fruits and metal weights onto either pan of the big scale, then press WEIGH to test your idea. If one side is heavier the scale tips and your stickman helper reacts, so think again and try once more. Use the free hint bulb to reveal a fruit weight when you get stuck.

  • 40 balance puzzles from easy sums to tricky clue chains
  • Deduce hidden fruit weights from balanced clue scales
  • Free hints and a level map to replay any puzzle
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
