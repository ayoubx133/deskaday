About the game

A stickman hosts a funny shadow show. Tap the shadow that the prop on stage really casts, find which prop made the big shadow, or rotate and flip the prop until it matches its shadow. Some shadows are mirrored or missing a tiny part, so look closely. Answer fast for a speed bonus and more stars.

  • 40 shadow puzzles in three fun modes
  • Over 20 cartoon props like rockets, cats and castles
  • Speed bonus stars and free hints
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
