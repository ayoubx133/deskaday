About the game

Explore a busy little harbor town full of boats, market stalls, fountains and tiny stick people. Drag to look around, pinch or tap the zoom buttons to get closer, and tap the tiny objects shown on your list. Stuck? The magnifier hint flies right to one hidden object. Find everything with few misses to earn three stars.

  • Pan and pinch zoom a detailed miniature town
  • 30 levels plus endless seek and find fun
  • Free magnifier hints, level map and 6 seeker outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
