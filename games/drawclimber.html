About the game

Race your stickman's buggy against a rival to the finish flag. Draw any shape in the pad at the bottom and both wheels turn into that shape right away. Round wheels are fast on flat ground and hills, while a long stick helps you climb stairs and walls. Redraw whenever the track changes and finish far ahead for three stars.

  • 36 handmade race tracks with stairs, hills and gaps
  • Your drawing becomes the wheels instantly
  • Six riders with their own buggy colors
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
