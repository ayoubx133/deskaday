About the game

The stickman floats under a bunch of colorful balloons. Tap a balloon to pop it and float down, or tap the pump to add one and rise up. The wind blows a different way at each height, so pick the right height to drift past thorny bushes and cheeky birds. Collect the stars and land softly on the pad.

  • 30 sky puzzles with winds, birds and floating extras
  • Simple one tap controls for all ages
  • Six balloon and stickman styles to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
