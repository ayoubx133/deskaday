About the game

Sit face to face with a friend and share one tablet: the top half is RED and the bottom half is BLUE. Win best of three rounds in ten quick mini games like tug of war, fastest tap, sumo ball, quick draw, ping pong, balloon pop, hot potato, color match, air hockey and jump rope. Tap fast, react first or drag your paddle while your stickman pulls, pumps, jumps and cheers. Playing alone? Take on the CPU in 40 levels that get tougher as you go, earn coins and unlock fun outfits for your stickman.

  • 10 quick duel mini games for 2 players on one device
  • VS CPU mode with 40 levels of rising challenge
  • Funny stickmen and 6 unlockable outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
