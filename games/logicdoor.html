About the game

Three doors, three signs and one prize. Smiley signs always tell the truth, tongue out signs always fib, and some puzzles say only one sign is true. Use the clues, colours and your key to pick the right door, then watch the stickman walk in. Wrong doors hide silly rooms like a water bucket splash or a pillow avalanche.

  • 40 logic door puzzles, each with exactly one answer
  • Truth signs, fib signs, keys and locks
  • Funny surprise rooms and free hints
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
