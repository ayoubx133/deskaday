About the game

Professor Stick asks a riddle and the answer is hidden in the picture. Count the cats, find the key under a hat, feed the mouse, tap the odd one out or put numbers in order. Some riddles have a sneaky trick, so look closely before you tap. You have three hearts for each riddle and a free hint button.

  • 50 handmade picture riddles and tricks
  • Tap, drag and move things to find hidden answers
  • Free hints, level map and six professor outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
