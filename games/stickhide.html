About the game

The seeker is counting to ten. Pick a disguise like a lampshade, leaves, a teddy suit or a statue pose, then tap a hiding spot that matches the room. Look at the room carefully: a lamp hides best among lamps and a statue among statues. The seeker checks every spot in a silly way, so only a clever disguise stays hidden.

  • 30 handmade rooms with smart disguise puzzles
  • Funny seeker checks and quick retries
  • Free hints, level map and six stickman outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
