About the game

Sneak a stretchy rubber arm into a cartoon toy bank vault and grab the bags of play coins. Laser beams switch on and off to the beat of the music, so watch the beat display and slip through at the right moment. Lay your arm across pressure plates to turn laser colours off, and open the safes by circling your hand around the dial to match the code on the sticky note. Sweeping turrets and moving beams make every level a new little heist puzzle.

  • 36 handmade laser puzzles with a level map
  • Lasers that blink to the beat, pressure plates and dial locks
  • Free hints that show the path, plus 6 agent skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
