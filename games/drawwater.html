About the game

A thirsty stickman waits next to his empty glass. Draw lines with your finger, then tap POUR and watch the water flow out of the tap, roll along your lines and bounce off shelves. Fill the glass up to the red line to make him cheer, and keep the drops away from the sleepy cat. Water, orange juice, lemonade, milk, cocoa, berry juice and bouncy candy beads all flow a little differently, and some levels have two glasses to fill at once.

  • 32 handmade water physics puzzles
  • Juice, milk, cocoa and bouncy candy beads
  • Free hints, level map and 6 stickman skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
