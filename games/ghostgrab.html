About the game

Your teddy, toy car and rubber duck are lost in a cute haunted dollhouse at night. Drag your stickman's glowing rubber arm through the dark rooms, up through the floors and around the furniture to bring every toy back. Friendly ghosts float around with flashlights, so stay out of their beams or they giggle BOO and you try again. Watch out for sticky cobwebs and use the free hint if a room gets tricky.

  • 36 handmade night puzzles with a level map
  • Dark rooms that light up around your glowing hand
  • Friendly ghosts with flashlights, plus 6 pajama skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
