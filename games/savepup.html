Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Draw one wall to keep the buzzing bees away from the puppy for 10 seconds.

About the game

Draw a single line with your finger and it drops into the scene as a solid wall. The bees fly out of the hive and buzz around for 10 seconds, so make sure your wall keeps them away from the puppy. Use less ink to earn three stars, and tap the light bulb whenever you want a hint. Later levels add wind, moving hives, ledges, pits and a sleepy kitten to protect too.

  • 36 handmade draw puzzles with real physics
  • Free hints, instant retry and a level map
  • Six stickman outfits with matching crayon colors
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
