About the game

Swipe left and right to guide your stickman tamer down the road to the castle. Run through the best gates to add more monsters or double your team, and dodge the red gates and wobbly jelly blocks. At the castle the boss team is waiting for a giant tug of war, so tap as fast as you can to pull them over the line. Collect coins on the way and unlock new tamer outfits.

  • Pick smart gates to grow a huge monster team
  • Tap fast in a funny tug of war boss battle
  • 11 cute monster types, level map and 6 outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
