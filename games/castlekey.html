About the game

A sleepy dragon guards the key on a pile of gold. Drag the hero's stretchy arm to snatch the key without waking it, then carry it to the cell lock to free the princess and the knight. Watch out: every big snore blows your arm away, and bats and swinging chandeliers get in the way. 32 handmade castle levels with free hints.

  • 32 castle puzzles in two steps: grab the key, open the lock
  • Snore gusts push your stretchy arm around
  • Unlock 6 hero skins from squire to king
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
