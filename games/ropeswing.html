About the game

Swipe across a vine to cut it and send the stickman flying through the jungle. Time each cut so he swings, soars and lands on the safe wooden platform. Golden rings catch him with a new vine, red mushrooms bounce him high and soft cacti make him say ouch. Grab all three bananas on the way for a perfect score.

  • 30 jungle swing puzzles with real rope physics
  • Rings, bouncy mushrooms and cute cacti to plan around
  • Free hints that tell you when to cut
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
