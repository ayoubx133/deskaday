About the game

Tap a candy hook and your stickman's sticky toy hand shoots out and sticks to it. Tap the next hook to stretch on from there and build a gummy path through the candy factory to the giant lollipop. Hooks ride on conveyor belts and spinning gears, candy presses stomp up and down and gumball cannons fire on a beat. Grab wrapped candies along the way for three stars, and tap your stickman any time to pull back and try another route.

  • 36 handmade tap and stretch puzzles
  • Moving hooks, candy presses and gumball cannons
  • Free hints that number every hook, plus 6 sweet skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
