About the game

A cheeky raccoon is sniffing around the picnic and wants the sandwiches, cupcakes and watermelon. Draw fences with your finger to wrap it in a cage or wall it off from every snack. Rocks, bushes, logs and the pond help you save ink. As soon as your first fence is done the raccoon dashes for the nearest snack and finds its way around every new fence, so close the gaps quickly. Use less ink for three stars.

  • 32 handmade trap and fence puzzles
  • A smart raccoon that finds its way around fences
  • Free hints, level map and 6 picnic skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
