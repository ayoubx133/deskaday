Tap the magnets to pull crates, keys and the robot buddy through the maze.

About the game

Every magnet on the wall pulls all the metal in its row or column, so crates slide, keys unlock doors and the little robot zooms along. Steer the robot onto its charging pad next to the stickman. Fill holes with crates, open doors with keys and later flip the switch to make the magnets push instead of pull. Solve each maze in the fewest moves for three stars.

  • 30 sliding magnet mazes from easy to brain bending
  • Undo, free hints and instant retry
  • Six stickman engineer outfits to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
