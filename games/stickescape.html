About the game

Tap things in the room to search drawers, lift the rug, swing the painting and open boxes to find items and clues. Drag items from your bag onto the room to use them, or drop one item on another to combine them, like a magnet on a string or batteries in a flashlight. Crack keypad codes, color locks, shape locks, arrow locks, dials, switches and picture puzzles to unlock the door. Stuck? Tap the free hint bulb to see the next step, then watch the stickman cheer and walk out of the room.

  • 30 handmade escape rooms from easy to tricky
  • Combine items and crack many kinds of locks
  • Free hints and 6 fun stickman skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
