Three stickmen tell their story. Spot the clue and find who is fibbing!

About the game

Every case has three stickmen and one silly fib. Read what each one says, then look closely for clues like muddy shoes, cake crumbs, wet hair or paint on the hands. Tap the stickman who is not telling the truth to close the case. Use the free hint when you need a tip, earn coins and dress up your detective.

  • 40 handmade detective cases with clever visual clues
  • Free hints, level map and three stars per case
  • Six detective outfits to unlock with coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
