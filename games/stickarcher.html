Pull back the toy bow and pop balloons with suction-cup arrows in 40 levels.

About the game

Drag back anywhere to aim the stickman archer's toy bow, watch the dotted path and the power bar, then let go to shoot a suction-cup arrow. Arrows fly with gravity and wind, bounce off metal plates, stick to wooden boards and targets, and pop every balloon in their way. Clear each level by popping all the balloons, hitting the targets and flying through the hoops before your arrows run out. Earn coins for every win and unlock six fun archer skins.

  • 40 handmade levels with wind, hoops and moving targets
  • Trick shots that bounce off metal plates
  • Six archer skins with hats, capes and crowns
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
