About the game

Each room is full of traps: rolling rocks, slime pipes and rising water. Tap ropes, levers, fans, balloons and umbrellas in the right order so every trap goes off safely. When all the lights on the door turn green, the stickman walks out free. A wrong order ends in a funny bonk or splash, so think first and try again.

  • 30 handmade chain reaction rooms
  • Rocks, slime, water, fans, lifts, pits and buttons
  • Free hints, level map and six explorer outfits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
