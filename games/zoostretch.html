About the game

Be a kind zookeeper with a super stretchy arm. Pick up bananas, fish, leaves, bamboo and melon, then reach through the cage gaps to feed the right animal. Give the wrong snack and they say yuck, so plan your path. Tiptoe past the sleeping lion, dodge the sprinklers and the cheeky parrot. 32 handmade levels with free hints.

  • 32 feeding puzzles with order cards and cage gaps
  • Monkey, penguin, giraffe, panda and hippo to feed
  • Unlock 6 keeper skins with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
