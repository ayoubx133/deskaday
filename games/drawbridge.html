Draw a bridge or ramp so the stickman's car reaches the finish flag.

About the game

Draw one line and it turns into a solid bridge. Lift your finger and the stickman drives his little car across gaps, rivers, steps and seesaws toward the flag. Plan your line so the car does not splash into the water, flip over or get stuck. Use less ink for three stars, tap the light bulb for a hint and retry instantly.

  • 36 handmade bridge puzzles with real car physics
  • Seesaws, crates, rocks, steps and big gaps
  • Six drivers with their own car and crayon colors
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
