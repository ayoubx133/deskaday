About the game

Hold anywhere to shoot a rope to the nearest hook and swing like a pendulum. Let go at the right moment to fly through the sky, flip in the air and grab the next hook. Bounce on trampolines, dodge floating rocks and collect three stars on your way to the finish gate. Win coins to unlock fun stickman looks.

  • 40 swinging levels with trampolines, moving hooks and floating rocks
  • Smooth rope physics, mid air flips and funny tumbles
  • 6 stickman skins with their own colorful ropes
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
