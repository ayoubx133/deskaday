About the game

You are a stretchy superhero stickman with a rubber arm that can reach anywhere. Drag the glove along a clever path to rescue scared kittens, catch kittens that jump off wobbly signs, grab runaway balloons and lift kids off high balconies. Heavy rescues need an anchor, so loop your arm around a lamp post first. Dodge wet paint, buzzing bees and sprinkler jets, and use the free hint whenever you get stuck.

  • 36 handmade rescue puzzles with a level map
  • Loop poles, catch falling kittens and chase balloons
  • Free hints that show the path, plus 6 hero skins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
